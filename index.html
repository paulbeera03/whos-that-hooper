Name today's hooper in 3 shots. Stuck? Unlock up to 3 more hints.

    Player data from Basketball-Reference via an open dataset, current through the 2026–27 season. A new hooper drops at midnight.

    How to play

    1. Every day there's one mystery NBA player, from the 1950s to right now.
    2. You get his silhouette and one opening clue. The silhouette is drawn from his listed height and weight, so a 7-foot center looks nothing like a 6-foot guard. Legends strike their signature move, like a skyhook or a one-legged fadeaway.
    3. You have 3 shots to name him. Pick a name from the dropdown and hit Shoot.
    4. Stuck? Unlock up to 3 hints: scouting report (including what he's known for), résumé, then his signature team.
    5. Miss all 3, or tap Reveal answer, and he's revealed. Fewer shots and fewer hints make a better score to share.

    Difficulty rotates daily: Easy, Medium, Hard. Practice mode (the arrows button) gives you unlimited random players that don't count toward your streak.
