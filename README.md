# Who's Dat Hooper? 🏀

A daily NBA silhouette guessing game, inspired by "Who's That Pokémon?" and Wordle.

**Play:** https://whosdathooper.io/

- One mystery player per day, from the 1950s to today
- 3 shots to name him, up to 3 hints, or reveal the answer
- Confetti reveal and a collectible trading card with career stats
- Streaks and stats are saved in your browser; share your result to X, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook, Reddit or WhatsApp

Player data: [Basketball-Reference](https://www.basketball-reference.com) via the open
[bball-reference-datasets](https://github.com/sumitrodatta/bball-reference-datasets) project.
Font: [TikTok Sans](https://github.com/tiktok/TikTokSans) (SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/OFL.txt`).
